Commands

move(right)
Move one tile: right, left, up, or down. move() defaults to right.
plant(wheat)
Plant wheat, tomato, or cucumber on an empty tile, using one seed. Wheat seeds are unlimited.
harvest()
Collect a grown plant. An immature plant is removed without a reward.
reset()
Return to the upper-left tile, (0, 0).
while(True):
Repeat the indented block until you press Stop.
while(False):
Skip the indented block.

One statement per line. Tab inserts four spaces; Shift+Tab removes indentation. Press Escape then Tab to leave the editor. Commands and loop checks each pause for 500 ms.

while(True):
    plant(wheat)
    harvest()
    move()

Blank lines and full-line # comments are allowed. Runs continue from the last position. Edge moves stay on the field.
